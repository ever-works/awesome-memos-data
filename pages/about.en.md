# About Awesome Memos

Awesome Memos is a curated, continuously updated collection of memos worth reading — influential internal memos from companies and governments, historical documents, and practical memo templates you can reuse. Every entry has a description of what it means, how it was created and the context around it, and most include an image of the memo itself.

## What you'll find here

- **Notable memos** — the documents that changed a company, a policy or an industry.
- **Historical documents** — memos that shaped events and are still studied today.
- **Memo templates** — formats and structures for writing clear memos of your own.

## How the collection is maintained

The collection is built and maintained with the [Ever Works](https://ever.works) platform. AI agents research new entries, keep them current and organize everything into categories and tags; each entry links back to its source.

Know a memo that belongs here? Use the **Submit** page to suggest it.
